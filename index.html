Questions

Before you ask

Why PETG and not PLA?

PLA softens and slowly creeps under sustained heat and load — exactly what a working AI machine produces, and no way to treat a tool someone relies on daily. PETG is heat-stable and tougher. PETG-CF goes further: stiffer, with a matte, machined look.

How long until my order ships?

Everything is printed to order — no warehouse shelf. Small orders typically print and ship within a few business days; towers and customs get a timeline in the quote before you pay anything.

What if something doesn't fit?

Every rack module is fit-tested against the real machine before it's listed, and every daily-living aid is tested in real hands. If what you receive doesn't fit the job it's listed for, we make it right — reprint or refund.

Can I print and sell these myself?

File purchases are personal-use: print freely for your own rig. Selling prints requires a commercial license (from $99 per design family) — it's how the designs stay ours and the quality stays controlled.

Is this the fitness company?

Same founder, sibling business. TSH Studios is the 3D printing arm of Time Strong Health — the TSH Products page here is the studio shelf for TSH clients, caregivers, and clinics. For in-home fitness and wellness services, visit timestronghealth.com.
