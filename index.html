Questions

Before you ask

Why PETG and not PLA?

PLA softens and slowly creeps under sustained heat and load — no way to treat a tool someone relies on daily. PETG is heat-stable and tougher.

How long until my order ships?

Everything is printed to order — no warehouse shelf. Small orders typically print and ship within a few business days; larger or custom orders get a timeline in the quote before you pay anything.

What if something doesn't fit?

Every daily-living aid is tested in real hands before it's listed. If what you receive doesn't fit the job it's listed for, we make it right — reprint or refund.

Is this the fitness company?

Same founder, sibling business. TSH Studios is the 3D printing arm of Time Strong Health — the TSH Products page here is the studio shelf for TSH clients, caregivers, and clinics. For in-home fitness and wellness services, visit timestronghealth.com.
